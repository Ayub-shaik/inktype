# InkType — monetization

## Positioning
Consumer + creator utility. Not enterprise diagramming (that’s Lucid/Miro). Price like CapCut / Canva mobile: low friction free tier, clear Pro unlocks.

## Pricing sketch (INR + USD)
- **Free:** unlimited typing, 3 templates, watermarked PNG, 1 OCR/PDF extract per day
- **Pro (~$6/mo or $49/yr):** all templates, clean exports, SVG + `.excalidraw`, saved boards, share links, higher OCR limits
- **Lifetime early-bird:** limited founding seats

## Growth loops
1. Watermarked PNGs that say “made with InkType”
2. Share links that open read-only preview → “Remix in InkType”
3. Template marketplace later (creator revenue share)

## Cost control
- Live path: $0 inference
- AI structure: short prompts, cached, Pro-gated volume
- OCR: tesseract free; cloud OCR Pro-only

## Compliance
- Uploads processed ephemerally on free; Pro saved boards encrypted at rest
- No silent training on private boards without opt-in
