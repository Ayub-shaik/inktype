# InkType — product brief

## One-liner
Live text → templated hand-drawn diagram, optimized for phones.

## Who pays
- Students turning lecture notes into study slides
- Engineers sketching flows for Slack/LinkedIn
- Creators who want “Excalidraw look” without learning Excalidraw
- Teams who want a shareable visual from a pasted brief

## Core loop
1. Pick a template (or start typing and pick later)
2. Type — preview updates word by word
3. Optionally upload a photo/PDF of notes — text lands in the editor
4. Switch templates mid-flight — content remaps into new slots
5. Export PNG (free watermarked / Pro clean) or share link (Pro)

## Non-goals (v1)
- Full freehand drawing editor (use Excalidraw for that)
- Multiplayer whiteboard (phase 2 via excalidraw-room)
- Auto-post to social networks

## Brand
- Name: **InkType**
- Feel: paper, ink, calm dark chrome around a bright “page”
- Motto: *Type it. Ink it. Ship it.*
